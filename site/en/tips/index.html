Save a paper with Zotero Connector

Zotero Connector reads the article in your browser and saves its details to Zotero. Zotero2Notion then syncs that record to your Notion database. Before you begin, install the plugin and connect a database.

  1. Install the browser extension.

    For Chrome, Firefox, or Edge, get the extension for your browser from the Zotero download page. On Safari, turn on the extension bundled with Zotero in Safari Settings → Extensions.

  2. Save from the article page.

    Open the Zotero desktop app. In your browser, visit the article’s abstract or publication page, then click Save to Zotero in the toolbar. Use the save popup to select My Library or one of its collections. Zotero’s guide to saving from the web

  3. Find the same paper in Notion.

    Check the title and authors in Zotero. Leave Zotero open, then look for that title in your connected Notion database. An attached PDF stays in Zotero, and the bibliographic fields you mapped appear in Notion.

Where did the paper stop?

Missing from Zotero? Open the desktop app and restart your browser. Troubleshoot the Connector connection

Present in Zotero but missing from Notion? Check that you saved a regular item to your personal library. Group libraries and standalone PDF attachments are excluded. In the plugin settings, confirm the selected database and review Check properties.

Make your own note template

If you ask the same questions each time you read a paper, put them in a template. When the plugin creates the paper’s page, it fills in its details and leaves room for your answers.

  1. Create a template to edit.

    In Zotero, open Settings → Zotero2Notion → Templates. Click New beside Edit template, or select Paper review and click Duplicate to start from a copy. Enter “My reading note” in Name.

  2. Paste the example into the Markdown editor.

    [title], [authors], and [year] pull in the paper’s title, authors, and publication year. Change the headings that begin with ## to questions you want to answer. Your edits save automatically.

    To include notes attached to the Zotero item, put [zotero_notes] once on a line of its own. Imported notes continue to sync. Write your personal notes outside their toggles.

  3. Preview the result and set the default.

    Click Preview to see the selected paper’s title and authors in place. If no paper is selected, the preview uses sample data. Choose “My reading note” under Default template, then save a new paper and open its Notion page. Edits to an outline preserve the content of pages you have already written.

Use a different outline by collection

Open Template rules → Add rule, choose Collection, then pick your collection and its template. The rule also covers its subcollections. To match by Tag, enter the exact tag name. If several rules match, the first one in the list wins.

Start with the Notion database template

To bring over example pages and database views, open the public Journal DB template and click Duplicate. Choose the workspace where you created your token. In your copy, try adding a view grouped by Status to track your reading.

In the plugin settings, click Refresh, select the copied database, and click Use. Review Check properties to confirm the field mapping. The plugin will sync papers to this database and use your Markdown template for the body of each new paper’s page.
